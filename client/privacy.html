Privacy Policy

Letschat Africa · Last updated: October 2026

Letschat Africa is a chat app with private and group chats, voice and video calls, status updates, a marketplace and games. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, phone number and/or email, profile photo and "about" text, from phone sign-in or Google sign-in (handled by Firebase Authentication).
  • Your content: messages, photos, files, voice notes, polls, status updates and marketplace listings you send or post.
  • Contacts you choose to share: if you pick contacts to find friends, the phone numbers you select are sent to our server only to check who is registered. We do not keep your address book.
  • Notifications: a push subscription for your device, if you turn notifications on.
  • Technical data: your connection to our server (for example IP address) and basic error information.

Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only for calls, photos, video and voice notes, and only when you use them.
  • Storage: to keep photos, your chats cache and backup files on your device.
  • Contacts: only the contacts you pick, when you tap the contact picker.

How we use it

To run the app: deliver messages and calls, show your profile and status to others, match you with friends, send notifications and keep the service safe. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see your data

Messages are visible to the people in the chat. Your name, photo and status are visible to people you chat with. Our service providers store and process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in), Supabase (database), and our hosting providers. Disappearing messages, status updates and listings are removed automatically after their set time.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account any time in the app (Tools → Delete account) or at the account deletion page. Deleting removes your profile, chats you were in privately and the messages you sent.

Children

Letschat Africa is not for children under 13.
