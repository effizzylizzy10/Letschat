Delete your account

In the app: open Tools, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account is removed straight away.

Can't open the app? Email from the address or send us the phone number you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We will remove it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, username, phone, email, photo and about text
  • Your private chats and all messages you sent
  • Your status updates and marketplace listings
  • Your notification subscriptions

Messages you sent in group chats are removed too. Other members keep the group.
